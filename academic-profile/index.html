About Me

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Economics and Finance at Tsinghua University, with expected graduation in June 2028. My research experience combines literature reviews, financial-data construction, and analysis of financing mechanisms.

CORPORATE FINANCE2026

Private credit & AI infrastructure

Examined how AI infrastructure is financed through debt, private credit, special-purpose vehicles, and leases. Case work considered contracts, collateral, refinancing needs, and the allocation of losses.

Contribution Financing-mechanism analysis, literature synthesis, and proposed event-study and stress-testing approaches.

  • Case studies
  • Research design

Methods Python and SQLite for data processing; checks for duplicate keys, field consistency, and date coverage; construction of adjusted prices, returns, and market capitalisation.
